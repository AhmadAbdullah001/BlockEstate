# Product Vision

BlockEstate reduces friction in buying and selling property by combining a marketplace, physical verification, document review, communication, negotiation, a lawyer marketplace, and transaction management.

Traditional portals usually stop at search, listing, and contact. BlockEstate aims to support: Submit -> Verify -> Publish -> Discover -> Contact -> Negotiate -> Agree -> Choose Lawyer -> Transaction Workspace -> Legal Process -> Closing.

One person uses one account and may buy, sell, save properties, contact sellers, make offers, and participate in transactions. Privileged roles are USER, LAWYER, INSPECTOR, and ADMIN; buyer and seller are not roles.

AI may assist with document extraction, search, descriptions, and risk signals, but it is never final legal authority. Government registration, title transfer, notarization, recording, and other legally required external processes remain outside the platform. Blockchain is future work and is not implemented in this phase.
