# Business Rules

1. One user can buy and sell.
2. Buyer and seller are not account types.
3. Required verification precedes verified publication.
4. Physical inspection is performed by an authorized inspector.
5. Verification stages and failures are visible and explainable.
6. Negotiation precedes transaction creation.
7. Lawyers are verified professionals who guide legal workflow.
8. Documents, payments, and messaging are first-class entities.
9. Admin manages platform operations.
10. AI assists people and never replaces legal authority.
11. Blockchain is deferred, but future integrations must remain possible.
