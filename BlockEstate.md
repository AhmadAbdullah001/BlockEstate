# BlockEstate
### A Trusted Property Buying and Selling Platform

## 1. Project Overview

**BlockEstate** is a digital real-estate platform designed to make buying and selling properties **safer, more transparent, and more trustworthy**.

The platform connects **property sellers, buyers, verification agents, and lawyers** in one ecosystem.

A major problem in real estate is the lack of trust and transparency. Buyers often do not know whether a property is legally owned by the seller, whether it has outstanding loans or dues, or whether there are other legal issues associated with it.

BlockEstate addresses these problems by introducing a **property verification system, professional agents, secure advance payments, and access to legal professionals**.

---

## 2. Main Users

BlockEstate will have four primary types of users:

1. **Sellers** – People who want to list and sell their properties.
2. **Buyers** – People looking for properties to purchase.
3. **Verification/Deal Agents** – Professionals who verify properties and assist buyers during negotiations.
4. **Lawyers** – Legal professionals who provide property-related legal assistance.

---

## 3. Seller Flow

### Step 1: Seller Login

The seller creates an account or logs into the BlockEstate platform.

After login, the seller is redirected to the **Seller Dashboard**.

### Step 2: Seller Dashboard

The dashboard provides the seller with:

- Profile information
- Previously listed properties
- Property selling history
- Verification status of properties
- Current property listings
- Option to list a new property

### Step 3: List a New Property

The seller can add a new property by providing information such as:

- Property type
- Property location
- Property address
- Expected price
- Property area
- Property images/videos
- Ownership documents
- Purchase information
- Other relevant legal documents

Initially, every newly listed property will be marked as:

> **Unverified**

### Step 4: Pay Verification Fee

Before the property can receive a verified status, the seller must pay a **property verification fee** through the BlockEstate platform.

Once the payment is completed, a BlockEstate verification agent is assigned to the property.

## 4. Property Verification Process

The verification process is one of the most important features of BlockEstate.

A BlockEstate agent will investigate the property and verify the information provided by the seller.

### Ownership Verification

The agent checks:

- Who legally owns the property
- Whether the seller is the actual owner
- Ownership history
- When and how the current owner acquired the property

### Financial Verification

The agent checks whether:

- There is an outstanding property loan
- The property is mortgaged
- Any bank or financial institution has a claim on the property
- Property taxes or other dues are pending

### Legal Verification

The agent checks for potential legal issues, including:

- Existing disputes
- Court cases
- Ownership conflicts
- Other relevant legal records
- Criminal or fraud-related concerns associated with the property or transaction, where legally permissible and relevant

### Physical Verification

The BlockEstate agent physically visits the property to verify:

- Whether the property actually exists
- Whether the property matches the submitted information
- Basic physical condition
- Location and address
- Whether there are visible ownership or occupancy issues

### Verification Result

After completing the investigation, the agent updates the property's verification status.

Possible statuses include:

- 🟡 **Unverified** – Verification has not yet been completed.
- 🟢 **Verified** – The property and submitted information have passed BlockEstate's verification process.
- 🔴 **Flagged / Potentially Fake** – Significant inconsistencies or concerns have been identified.
- ⚠️ **Verification Failed** – The property could not satisfy the required verification criteria.

The platform should also display **what was verified and when**, rather than presenting verification as an absolute guarantee of legal ownership.

## 5. Buyer Flow

### Step 1: Buyer Registration/Login

The buyer creates an account or logs into BlockEstate.

After login, the buyer can browse available properties.

### Step 2: Discover Properties

The platform recommends properties based on the buyer's location and preferences.

The buyer can search and filter properties using:

- Location
- Price range
- Property type
- Size
- Number of rooms
- Verification status
- Distance
- Other preferences

Verified properties can receive greater visibility and a clear **Verified** badge.

### Step 3: View Property Details

The buyer can open a property listing and view:

- Property images/videos
- Location
- Asking price
- Property details
- Seller-provided information
- Verification status
- Verification date
- Available verification information
- Agent information, where applicable

---

## 6. Hiring an Agent

If a buyer is interested in a property, they can choose to **hire a BlockEstate agent**.

The agent acts as an intermediary between the buyer and seller.

The agent can:

- Contact the seller on behalf of the buyer
- Arrange property visits
- Communicate offers
- Help negotiate the price
- Coordinate communication between both parties
- Assist with the transaction process

The buyer pays the applicable **agent service fee** according to the platform's pricing model.

---

## 7. Deal Confirmation

If the buyer and seller agree on the price and both parties decide to proceed with the transaction, the deal moves to the **Deal Confirmation** stage.

At this stage, the platform records the agreed terms and begins the secure payment process.

---

## 8. Advance Payment / Escrow System

The buyer may be required to pay an **advance amount** through BlockEstate.

Instead of transferring the money directly to the seller, the payment is held securely by the platform or an appropriate regulated escrow/payment partner until the agreed conditions are satisfied.

### Why This Is Important

This protects the buyer from immediately losing the advance payment if the transaction fails due to an agreed-upon reason.

Example:

**Buyer → BlockEstate / Regulated Escrow → Seller**

If the transaction successfully progresses according to the agreed terms, the funds can be released according to the agreement.

If the deal is cancelled under a valid refund condition, the advance can be returned to the buyer.

The exact refund and release rules should be clearly defined before the buyer makes the payment.

- Professional qualifications

- Consultation fee

- Contact/booking option

### Example

A buyer who is interested in purchasing a property can hire a lawyer to review:

- Property documents
- Ownership documents
- Sale agreements
- Loan/mortgage information
- Legal disputes
- Other relevant transaction documents

This creates an additional layer of trust and legal support.

## 12. The Problem BlockEstate Solves

Traditional property transactions can involve several risks:

- Fake property listings
- Fraudulent sellers
- Unclear ownership
- Hidden loans or mortgages
- Outstanding property dues
- Legal disputes
- Lack of professional negotiation
- Unsafe advance payments
- Difficulty finding trustworthy property lawyers
- Lack of transparency between buyers and sellers

**BlockEstate brings these services together into one platform.**

Instead of the buyer having to independently coordinate with sellers, agents, lawyers, and financial intermediaries, BlockEstate provides a unified workflow.
